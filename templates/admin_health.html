Sync

The cron syncs every 30 minutes during the day, but only does work when something's actually live or a full sync hasn't run yet today — otherwise it no-ops. Use this to force a full sync right now instead of waiting (e.g. right after a gameweek finishes, or if a match's stats look stale).

Editing Lock

When locked, the public can't record picks or trades; you (logged in) still can. Use this to freeze edits outside the live draft window.

Freezing is automatic once GW38 finishes. FPL reuses league ids between seasons, so a finished season must never be re-read from the feed.
