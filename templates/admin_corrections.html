Draft picks ({{ picks|length }})

A discovery pick is recorded as free text, because the player isn't in the Premier League yet. Once he joins, link it to his real player record — that link is what gives him the 4-year discovery keeper clock instead of a 3-year waiver one. Enter his FPL id (the fpl_id shown on the Players tab). Nothing is ever matched by name automatically: FPL stores short names and managers call out full ones, and a wrong link hands someone another manager's keeper.
