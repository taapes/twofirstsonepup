Season Rollover commissioner

Advance to a new season once the new FPL draft league exists. This runs in two steps: first it syncs the new league (teams, standings, schedule), then it asks you to confirm which new team is which person. Only after you confirm does it carry logins and keeper clocks forward (years − 1 for kept players), snapshot the player pool for the ineligible-player rule, and make the new season current. The previous season stays current — and the site keeps working — right up until that confirmation, so stopping halfway is safe.

The mapping step is not busywork: FPL issues every manager a new entry id each season and team names change, so nothing can pair them reliably. Guessing it is what broke the 26/27 rollover — every login and every keeper clock was lost silently.
