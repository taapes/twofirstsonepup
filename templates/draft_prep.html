Predicted keepers & pick ledger

A thin margin means the model was nearly indifferent between two legal sets — those managers could easily keep someone else, so treat their leftovers as genuinely uncertain.

Net is picks traded in or out, and it is the only thing that moves a squad off 15: your own slots are 15 − keepers and your keepers are keepers, so those two always cancel regardless of how many you keep. A manager who is net positive holds more picks than he has room for and the extras lapse; net negative means he finishes short and fills from free agency.
